## Use

The game's printed manual, as a 43-page image scan (16,998,773 bytes) with no text layer, so it
is read by eye and cited by page. It describes the screens and the player's choices: character
creation, the estate, War Planning (pages 36 to 38), tournaments, castle assaults and the
dragon quest. It gives few numbers and no formulas, so it serves as a lead and as the name the
game gives a concept.
