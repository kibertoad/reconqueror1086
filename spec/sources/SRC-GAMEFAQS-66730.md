## Use

A player's guide written from play, not from the game's files. It covers character creation
and the fixed starting profiles, the estate economy (crop, forest and unit tables, staffing,
tax and population), tournaments and wagers, courtship, equipment prices and armour, castle
assaults (radar, secret doors, food, treasure, crossbows, retainers, weapon breakage), the end
conditions and the dragon quest. It is a lead for where to look in the executable and for how a
behaviour looks to the player. It gives no addresses, state tables, thresholds or timings, so
it neither confirms nor contradicts the executable's internal rules. The date it was written
was not recorded when it was read.

## Known errors

None known. Some of its claims have not been checked against the executable yet, and the rules
that cover them list them in their Open questions sections: that building the same
productivity buildings in a different order can give 90% instead of 95%, and that Chaunce
Norman's attributes and wealth are random. It gives each unit kind's price and upkeep at 50% and 100% productivity; the executable holds the same two sets and picks between them by FAME (RULE-ESTATE-001). Its weapon lengths are values measured in play, and
the rules for reach and damage come from the executable's combat rows instead.
