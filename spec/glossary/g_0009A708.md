# g_0009A708

A neutral name: the four-byte global at `0x0009A708` in BLD-GOG-EN. Dubbing's
full-screen callback reads it through DS, passes a nonzero value to sample
playback and then stores zero before replacing the screen [FND-UI-024]. Its
initialization and the identity of the represented sample remain untraced
(Q-UI-044).
