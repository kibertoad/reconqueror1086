# battle_units

The units of the field battle, a list of FMT-BATTLE-001, kept in the block the pointer at `0x000A9CB8` points to, with its count in the global at `0x000A9C90` [FND-BATTLE-004, FND-BATTLE-008]. It is kept in the order the constructor fills it: the player's units by category, then the foe's [FND-BATTLE-004].
