# g_0009F505

The six-byte selector/offset source read by the environment-array constructor
[FND-RNG-011]. Its first four bytes are an offset; the word at offset four is
loaded into FS and then ES for source reads. FND-RNG-012 identifies startup's
four-byte offset and two-byte selector stores through DS. Their incoming values
and full startup provenance remain under Q-RNG-001. This name does not equate
the source segment with DS.
