# character_attributes

The attributes of the 15 characters: 15 rows of `INT32[30]` in the order of `CHARACTR.DAT`, row 0 the player [FND-PERSON-002]. The game reaches row `r` through the list of row records the pointer at `0x0009A650` points to, and the pointer at offset `0x50` of the record [FND-PERSON-001].
