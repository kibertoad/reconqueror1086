## Observation

90 of the 99 `.RES` and `.LOW` archives in `CD:CONQUER` have a `Scenario` resource, and each is
exactly 568 bytes. The little-endian dwords at offsets 20, 24 and 28 equal the archive's number
of `TEX` resources, the number of `Blocks` records and the number of `SFXDEFS` records. The
dword at 40 is 1 and the dword at 44 is 32 in all 90. The pairs at offsets 48 and 52 are
(10, 20) in the 36 `MELEE*` archives, (15, 16) in `DEFEND0`, (9, 20) in `DEFEND1`, (8, 0) in
`DEFEND2` and 26 others, (9, 10) in one pair of archives and (7, 0) in 20.

## Interpretation

The counts size the scene's tables. Offsets 40 to 52 are the colour-map switch, count, distance
shift and fade colour that FND-VIEW-011 finds the drawing code reading.

## Alternatives

None known.

## How to reproduce

Decode the `Scenario` entry of each archive and compare its dwords with the entry counts of the
same archive.
