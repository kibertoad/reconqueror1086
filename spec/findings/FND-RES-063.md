## Interpretation

These analyzer extents locate body boundaries for reviewing historical
ranges. They do not establish what a historical author intended a partial
range to include, or the behavior, reachability or completeness of a reading.

## Alternatives

A last-byte coincidence does not prove intended scope. A partial range whose
scope is uncertain still requires whole-entry supersession. Adding body size
to the entry would lose separate tails and count gaps as code.

## How to reproduce

Use the source identities, frozen snapshot digests, source mappings, language
selections and read-only repeated-export procedure in FND-RES-062. Match each
entry and its complete separate body ranges against the exported metadata.
For INST.EXE first obtain and verify the unpacked form as FND-RES-066 requires.
Keep source files, raw exports, logs and databases local. No original function
or game is executed.
