## Observation

The signed word at actor block `+0x46` (the high word of the dword at `+0x44`) is 7 on 134 of
the 144 actor bases and 10 on the other ten. In each archive, `SFXDEFS` descriptors 7 and 10
(FMT-ASSAULT-003) both have tick count 3, interval 200 ms, flags `0x142`, map delta per tick
`(64, 0)` and surface delta per tick 5. The signed dwords at actor block `+0x24` and `+0x28`
are 0 on every actor base.

Each actor's four state blocks select descriptor 0 through the word at `+0x2E`. Descriptor 0
has tick count 2, flags 5, block step `+1` and no coordinate, surface or heading step. Its
interval is 192 ms on 80 actor bases and 200 ms on the other 64. The four state blocks have
angular division counts (block `+0x34`) 8, 4, 4 and 2.

## Interpretation

All shipped actors walk with the same movement effect: three ticks of 200 ms, each moving 64
units (a quarter cell) along the actor's heading. Their visual state changes use a separate
two-tick effect.

## Alternatives

None known.

## How to reproduce

Decode each archive's `Blocks` and `SFXDEFS` and group the actor bases by `+0x46` and `+0x2E`.
