## Observation

In the player's weapon path at `0x000559CA`..`0x00055A4E`, after the depth is compared with the
combat row's reach plus `0x40`, the test of behaviour bit `0x20` at `0x00055A02` is the only
test of that bit in the executable. When it is set, the path calls `0x0004C900`, which writes
the low word of the block's state target `+0x40` into the contacted map cell. In the 42 archives
of FND-ASSAULT-002, the placed kind-4 blocks with bit `0x20` have behaviour 33 or 35.

## Interpretation

Some scenery changes when the player's weapon reaches it, and is replaced by the block its state
target names.

## Alternatives

None known.

## How to reproduce

Open `0x00055A02` in the path from `0x000559CA` and follow the call to `0x0004C900`. For the
census, group the placed kind-4 blocks of each archive by behaviour bit `0x20`.
